# Getting started

## Off you go

1. Press {key:escapemenudialog} and open **Settings**. Komet's pages are in the **Komet** tab at the top.
2. Pick a [[preset]] under **Komet › Overview**. {key:escapemenudialog} closes the menu again.
3. Press {key:komet-hud-toggle}. The [[HUD]] at the top shows the [[frame time]].

Stutter shows as spikes in the frame time, not in the FPS.
